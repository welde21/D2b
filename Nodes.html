What a country node does

A node brings the D2P model to life in one country, using national data and national priorities.

Answers priority questions

Works with the ministry on the policy questions that matter most nationally.

Turns data into evidence

Analyses national data and synthesises evidence using common methods.

Supports policy dialogue

Produces policy briefs and convenes decision-makers and stakeholders.

Shares and learns

Contributes outputs and lessons to the network and learns from peers.

How nodes and the Hub work together

What the Secretariat provides

Common methods and standards, analytical and evidence-synthesis support, training and fellowships, the digital knowledge platform, and cross-country learning.

What each node provides

National leadership, access to national priorities and stakeholders, local expertise, and outputs that feed the shared knowledge library.

National data stays national.Data shown or linked on node pages remains owned and governed by the country concerned. The Hub links to national systems and does not replace them.

Join the network

The Hub welcomes African countries that want to strengthen how health data and evidence inform policy.

Step 1

Express interest

Contact the Secretariat to discuss your priorities and how the Hub could help.

Step 2

Identify a host

Agree a host institution, a coordinator and a link to the ministry of health.

Step 3

Establish the node

Adopt the common standards, agree a work plan and begin joint work.
