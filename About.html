Mission and vision

Our mission

To strengthen the capacity of African countries to transform health data, research evidence, implementation experience and regulatory information into actionable policies, regulatory decisions, programmes and improved health-system performance.

Our 2031 vision

A functioning regional network of about 8 to 12 African countries, supported by country D2P nodes, a regional Secretariat, a Governing Council, a Technical Advisory Committee, a digital knowledge platform, and a sustainable partnership and financing model.

Our guiding principle

Regional standards, national ownership.

Regional standards

Common methods, quality criteria and tools so that work in one country can be understood, compared and reused in another.

National ownership

Each country owns its data, sets its own priorities and takes its own decisions. The Hub supports and does not direct.

Complement, not replace

The Hub works alongside existing national, regional and continental health information and research systems.

The D2P model

Nine connected steps take health data all the way to policy and back again. Learning at the end improves the data at the start.

1Data

Routine, survey, research and regulatory data from national systems.

2Analysis

Quality checks, integration and analytics.

3Evidence

Synthesis of analysis, research and implementation experience.

4Policy options

Evidence turned into clear choices for decision-makers.

5Policy dialogue

Structured conversations with ministries, regulators and stakeholders.

6Decision or regulation

Policy, programme and regulatory decisions taken by countries.

7Implementation

Decisions put into practice in the health system.

8Monitoring

Delivery and performance tracked against agreed indicators.

9Learning

Lessons feed back to improve data, evidence and decisions.

What the Hub provides

  • Common methodologies and standards
  • Analytical capacity
  • Evidence synthesis
  • Knowledge translation and policy support
  • Capacity development
  • Digital knowledge infrastructure
  • Cross-country learning

What the Hub does not do

The Hub does not replace national data systems and does not take ownership of national data.

Countries decide what they share, with whom and under what terms. Any future data-sharing will follow national ownership, consent and privacy, and clear agreements.

Strategy 2026 to 2031: six pillars

The five-year strategy is organised around six pillars. Detailed activities and results will be published as the strategy is finalised.

Pillar 1

Data for Decision-Making

Help countries get the right data, in usable form, to the people who make decisions.

  • Data quality and integration approaches
  • Analytics support for priority policy questions
  • Using routine and survey data together
Pillar 2

Evidence Generation and Synthesis

Produce and combine evidence that answers the questions policymakers are asking.

  • Rapid reviews and evidence maps
  • Shared research priorities
  • Implementation research
Pillar 3

Evidence-to-Policy Translation

Turn evidence into clear policy options and support the dialogue that follows.

  • Plain-language policy briefs
  • Policy dialogues with stakeholders
  • Knowledge brokering
Pillar 4

Health Policy and Regulation

Support evidence-informed policy analysis and regulatory decisions.

  • Policy and regulatory analysis
  • Comparing regulatory approaches
  • Harmonization where countries choose it
Pillar 5

Capacity Development

Build lasting skills and institutions for using data and evidence.

  • Courses, fellowships and mentorship
  • Strengthening node institutions
  • Peer learning exchanges
Pillar 6

Digital Knowledge and Learning

Create a shared digital home for knowledge, tools and learning.

  • A digital knowledge platform
  • Communities of practice
  • Foundations for a future data hub

Governance

A simple structure keeps direction regional and delivery national.

Governing Council

Sets direction, approves the strategy and budget, and oversees performance.

Technical Advisory Committee

Advises on scientific and methodological quality and on standards.

Regional Secretariat

Coordinates work plans, standards, partnerships and the digital knowledge platform.

How a country joins

1. Express interest

Contact the Secretariat to discuss priorities and fit.

2. Identify a host

Agree a host institution, coordinator and link to the ministry of health.

3. Establish the node

Sign up to the common standards and begin joint work.

Joining criteria and process are draft and will be confirmed by the Governing Council.

Partners and financing

The Hub is built on partnership: national governments, research institutions, regional and continental bodies, and development partners. A sustainable financing model is part of the 2031 vision.
