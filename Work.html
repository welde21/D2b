Our work: six pillars, one pipeline

Our five-year strategy turns the D2P model into six areas of work. Each one supports country teams at specific steps between data and decisions, always under national ownership.

How the pillars fit the D2P model

The nine steps of the model run from data to learning. Each pillar strengthens the steps shown below.

PillarD2P steps it supports
1. Data for Decision-MakingData · Analysis
2. Evidence Generation and SynthesisAnalysis · Evidence
3. Evidence-to-Policy TranslationPolicy options · Policy dialogue
4. Health Policy and RegulationDecision or regulation · Implementation
5. Capacity DevelopmentAll steps
6. Digital Knowledge and LearningMonitoring · Learning

The mapping is indicative. In practice the pillars overlap and reinforce each other.

1

Data for Decision-Making

DataAnalysis

Objective

Help countries get the right data, in usable form, to the people who make decisions.

Why it matters

Routine and survey data are often fragmented, of uneven quality, or arrive too late to inform choices.

Key activities

  • Data quality and integration approaches
  • Analytics support for priority policy questions
  • Guidance on using routine and survey data together

Expected outcome by 2031

Partner institutions routinely use quality-checked data to answer priority policy questions.

Outcomes are draft until aligned with the results framework.Related resources
2

Evidence Generation and Synthesis

AnalysisEvidence

Objective

Produce and combine evidence that answers the questions policymakers are actually asking.

Why it matters

Decision-makers need timely, trustworthy summaries, not only individual studies.

Key activities

  • Rapid reviews and evidence maps
  • Shared research priorities across nodes
  • Implementation research

Expected outcome by 2031

A shared research agenda and a steady flow of syntheses tied to national policy questions.

Outcomes are draft until aligned with the results framework.Related resources
3

Evidence-to-Policy Translation

Policy optionsPolicy dialogue

Objective

Turn evidence into clear policy options and support the conversations that follow.

Why it matters

Evidence rarely changes policy without translation, trust and dialogue.

Key activities

  • Plain-language policy briefs
  • Policy dialogues with ministries and stakeholders
  • Knowledge brokering

Expected outcome by 2031

Policy briefs and dialogues are an established part of how nodes work with ministries.

Outcomes are draft until aligned with the results framework.Related resources
4

Health Policy and Regulation

Decision or regulationImplementation

Objective

Support evidence-informed policy analysis and regulatory decisions, and learning across borders.

Why it matters

Regulatory and policy information is scattered, and countries often repeat each other's work.

Key activities

  • Policy and regulatory analysis
  • Comparison of regulatory approaches
  • Support for harmonization where countries choose it

Expected outcome by 2031

Countries can compare and learn from each other's policy and regulatory experience.

Outcomes are draft until aligned with the results framework.Related resources
5

Capacity Development

All steps

Objective

Build lasting skills and institutions for using data and evidence.

Why it matters

Capacity is the difference between a platform that exists and one that is used.

Key activities

  • Courses, fellowships and mentorship
  • Strengthening node institutions
  • Peer learning exchanges

Expected outcome by 2031

Strong, sustainable national teams in every participating country.

Outcomes are draft until aligned with the results framework.Related resources
6

Digital Knowledge and Learning

MonitoringLearning

Objective

Create a shared digital home for knowledge, tools and learning.

Why it matters

A common platform lets lessons travel faster than the people who learned them.

Key activities

  • A digital knowledge platform and repository
  • Communities of practice
  • Foundations for a future data hub

Expected outcome by 2031

A working digital knowledge platform and an active community of practice across the network.

Outcomes are draft until aligned with the results framework.Related resources

What ties the work together

Country nodes deliver

Work is carried out with and through national D2P nodes in Ethiopia, Rwanda and Zambia, and later in other countries.

Common standards

Shared methods and quality criteria make work comparable and reusable across borders.

National ownership

Countries own their data and decisions. The Hub supports and does not direct.
