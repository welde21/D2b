Turning Health Data into Policy Action Across Africa

Regional standards, national ownership.

The Data to Policy (D2P) Africa Hub helps countries turn health data, research evidence, implementation experience and regulatory information into decisions that strengthen health systems.

Explore the modelBrowse resources

    Why the Hub

    Africa produces more health data and research than ever, but too little of it reaches the people who write policy and regulate medicines when they need it.

    Countries often work on the same questions separately, so lessons stay local and good practice travels slowly.

    The Hub connects national teams around shared methods while every country keeps ownership of its own data.

    What the Hub provides

    • Common methodologies and standards
    • Analytical capacity and evidence synthesis
    • Knowledge translation and policy support
    • Capacity development
    • Digital knowledge infrastructure
    • Cross-country learning

    What the Hub does not do

    It does not replace national data systems or own national data. It complements national, regional and continental systems. Countries decide what they share and on what terms.

    Six pillars for 2026 to 2031

    Each pillar covers one step in turning data into action. Together they make up the five-year strategy.

      Data Hub

      The Data Hub is in development. It will let participating countries share selected datasets and indicators through a common catalogue, under national ownership and clear data-sharing agreements.

      • National ownership of every dataset
      • Consent and privacy by design
      • Access tiers: public, registered, by request
      • Links to the owner's own system, not copies
